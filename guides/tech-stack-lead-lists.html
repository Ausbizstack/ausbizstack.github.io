How agencies build tech-stack lead lists from a list of domains

Who this is for: agencies and SaaS sales teams who sell a service tied to a technology ("we migrate WooCommerce stores", "we fix HubSpot setups", "we integrate Xero") and want a list of businesses that use it, with a published company contact.

Tool used: Tech Stack Detector – Bulk Website Technology Lookup (Apify Store, pay per enriched record). For Shopify stores specifically, see the Shopify App Detector & Theme Detector.

What you get per domain

  • detectedTechStack, techByCategory and techCount: about 150 signatures in 10 categories, including CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace), ecommerce (Shopify, WooCommerce), CRM and marketing (HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, Mailchimp), analytics and ad pixels, booking and POS, trades and field service, healthcare scheduling, and accounting tools
  • Role-based contacts only: roleEmails (info@, sales@, support@ and similar) and phones. Personal email addresses are filtered out by default
  • Email security basics (emailProvider, dmarcPolicy), AI search readiness (geoReadinessScore), and optional Australian ABN / GST checks for .au sites
  • leadOpportunityScore and short salesAngles to help prioritise

Step 1: Get the starting list of domains

Use a list you are allowed to use: your own CRM, an event attendee list with consent, an industry directory you can export, or the businesses in a niche you already research. The Actor enriches domains you give it; it doesn't discover companies.

Step 2: Run the enrichment

Open the Actor page, click Try for free, and paste the domains. JSON input:

{
  "targets": ["https://www.example-cafe.com.au", "example-dental.com", "example-plumbing.co.uk"],
  "crawlContactPages": true,
  "maxPagesPerDomain": 2,
  "auditAiGeoReadiness": true,
  "verifyAbn": true,
  "concurrency": 10
}
  • crawlContactPages and maxPagesPerDomain control how many contact/about pages are read for contacts and extra tech signals.
  • verifyAbn only matters for Australian sites; turn it off for other markets.

Step 3: Filter into a lead list

Export to CSV or Google Sheets and filter on detectedTechStack:

You sell Filter Useful extra column
WooCommerce to Shopify migrations contains WooCommerce techCount, geoReadinessScore
HubSpot or CRM implementation contains HubSpot, or no CRM in techByCategory roleEmails
Analytics and tracking setup no Google Analytics 4 and no Google Tag Manager phones
Booking system switches contains a competitor booking tool leadOpportunityScore
Email deliverability dmarcPolicy is none or missing emailProvider

Then sort by leadOpportunityScore and write the first line of each message from what you found ("I noticed your site runs WooCommerce with no analytics tag...").

Step 4: Keep outreach compliant

The Actor only returns role-based business contacts that the company has published. Before you email anyone, check the anti-spam law where the recipient is (for example the Spam Act 2003 in Australia, PECR and UK GDPR in the UK, CAN-SPAM in the US): identify yourself, make the message relevant to the business, and include a working unsubscribe.

Automate it with Make or n8n

  • Make: a Google Sheets Watch Rows trigger on a "domains" sheet, then the Apify app's Run an Actor (synchronous) and Get Dataset Items, then Add a Row in a "leads" sheet. Starter blueprint: templates/make/b2b-tech-stack-contact-abn-enricher.blueprint.json. See Apify's Make guide.
  • n8n: the Apify node's Run an Actor and get dataset, a Code or Filter node on detectedTechStack, then Google Sheets or your CRM node. Starter workflow: templates/n8n/b2b-tech-stack-contact-abn-enricher.workflow.json. See Apify's n8n guide.
  • Clay or any HTTP tool:

FAQ

How accurate is detection? It matches published signatures in the HTML, scripts, iframes, links and HTTP headers of the homepage and the pages it reads. Tools that leave no trace on public pages (for example a back-office CRM) can't be seen by any website-based detector.

Is estimatedMonthlySaaSSpendUsd a real figure? No. It is an indicative estimate from the detected tools, not a measured figure or what the company actually pays.
