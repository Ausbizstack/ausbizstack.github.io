Eligibility

Who can link?

Can link

  • Claude Platform (Console) organizations that Anthropic bills directly
  • Prepaid credit you bought, or monthly invoiced billing
  • Credits written into your agreement with Anthropic
  • Approved by an Owner, Admin or Billing member, once for the whole org
Sources: Claude Platform docs · Claude Help Center

Can't link

  • Claude subscriptions: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise
  • Claude via Bedrock, Vertex AI or Foundry
  • Console orgs paying through the AWS, Google Cloud or Azure marketplace
  • Monthly API credits from Max, Ultra or Team plans, and startup, VC or partner-program credits, can't pay
Sources: Offer linked billing (Help Center) · Use your account in a third-party app

Know before you link

  • Requests run in the platform's organization; only billing moves to you
  • Your ZDR, BAA, CMEK and IP-allowlist settings don't apply
  • Don't link if you need HIPAA, FERPA or CMEK coverage
  • Set a monthly spend limit per platform, and unlink any time in Console
Sources: Claude Platform docs · Anthropic announcement

For builders

Beta limits and test mode

Test mode

  • New apps start in testing
  • Only listed test users, up to 10, can link
  • Each test link must set a monthly limit of $50 or less
  • Going live needs a DNS TXT domain proof and Anthropic's review, unless your org has invoiced billing
Sources: Help Center · Publish your app (docs)

What's covered

  • /v1/messages and /v1/chat/completions
  • Choose who pays per request with billing_policy
  • Not yet: Message Batches API or Claude Managed Agents
  • Customer-paid requests can't use billable code execution (400)
Sources: Linked billing docs · Release notes, 17 Nov 2026

Beta caveats

  • Endpoints, parameters and behaviour can change during the beta
  • No webhook when a customer unlinks; you find out from a 403
  • 402 means a spend limit or no billable credit; 503 means retry with backoff
  • Requests share your org's rate limits, model access and service tier
Sources: Claude Platform docs · Help Center

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is Claude linked billing?

A beta feature of the Claude Platform, launched 17 November 2026. A customer links their Claude Platform (Console) organization to a third-party app once, and Anthropic bills that organization directly for the Claude API usage the app runs for them. No API key is shared, and the customer can set a monthly spend limit or unlink at any time.

Can I link a Claude Pro or Max subscription?

No. Claude subscription plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise) can't be linked, and the monthly API credits that come with Max, Ultra and Team plans can't pay for linked usage. You need a Claude Platform organization billed directly by Anthropic through prepaid credit or an invoice.

Can I link if I use Claude through AWS Bedrock, Vertex AI or Foundry?

No. Customers who get Claude through a cloud provider, and Console organizations that pay through the AWS, Google Cloud or Azure marketplace, can't use linked billing.

Who in my company has to approve the link?

A member of the Claude organization with the Owner, Admin or Billing role. One approval covers the whole organization, and the link stays active if that person leaves.

Do my zero-data-retention or HIPAA settings apply?

No. Linked requests run in the platform's organization, so your own IP allowlists, encryption keys, data retention (including ZDR), BAA and CMEK settings don't apply. Anthropic says not to link if you need HIPAA, FERPA or CMEK coverage during the beta.

What happens when my spend limit is reached?

Anthropic refuses the request rather than billing the platform (usually a 402 error). What happens next depends on the platform: some fall back to their own credits, others stop. Each listing on this page shows that behaviour where the platform documents it.

Is this tracker affiliated with Anthropic?

No. It's an independent resource by AusBizStack. Every listing links to Anthropic's own pages or the platform's own docs, blog or changelog.
